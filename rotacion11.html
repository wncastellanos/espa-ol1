¿Cómo escribo acentos?

  • Chromebook or Mac: press and hold the letter (a, e, i, o, u, n). A little menu pops up above it. Click the letter with the accent, or press its number.
  • Any computer: click in your answer box, then click a letter in the gray bar at the bottom of the screen: á é í ó ú ñ ¿ ¡
  • Windows: holding the key doesn't work, so use the gray bar.

Accents matter in Spanish: tú (you) and tu (your) are different words.
